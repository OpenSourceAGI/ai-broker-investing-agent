**Auto Investment Broker** combines specialized LLM agents to analyze markets,
debate strategies, and execute trades. It pairs real-time data processing with a
"Bull vs. Bear" debate engine and a dashboard that shows the reasoning behind
every decision — not just the outcome.

## Key features

- **Multi-agent architecture** — Fundamentals, News, Technical, and Risk Manager
  agents, each with a defined mandate.
- **"Bull vs. Bear" debates** — an automated argument over risk and reward
  before every trade, not a single opaque score.
- **Top trader leaderboards** — real-time tracking of top performers on
  ZuluTrade and Polymarket, with copy-trading signals.
- **Interactive dashboard** — per-agent reports, decision history, and technical
  charts.
- **Prediction-market correlation** — cross-platform arbitrage and correlation
  between event markets and equities.

## Agent teams

| Team | Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Analyst Team** | Gathers data: fundamentals, social sentiment, news, and technical analysis. |
| **Researcher Team** | Conducts "Bull vs. Bear" debates and assesses risk. |
| **Trader Agent** | Synthesizes the reports into a proposed trade. |
| **Portfolio Manager** | Final decision maker; manages risk and position sizing. |

**Strategies**: momentum (trend following), mean reversion, breakout
(volatility), and day-trading scalp.
